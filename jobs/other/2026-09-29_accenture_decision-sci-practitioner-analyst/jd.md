### What are we looking for?
- Total 3 to 5 years of experience; 2+ years in social media analytics or digital analytics
- Hands-on experience with social media analytics and listening tools (Sprinklr, Brandwatch, Meltwater, or similar)
- Proficiency in native analytics platforms: Meta Business Suite, LinkedIn Analytics, YouTube Studio, TikTok Analytics
- Advanced Excel or Google Sheets for data manipulation and reporting
- Solid understanding of social media KPIs: engagement rate, reach, impressions, sentiment score, SOV
- Ability to identify content trends and translate data into strategic recommendations
- Strong attention to detail and data quality mindset
- Good English communication and presentation skills
- Bachelor's degree in Marketing, Communications, Business, Statistics, or a related field
- Experience with Power BI or Tableau for social media dashboards
- Basic SQL for data extraction and manipulation
- Knowledge of SEO and its relationship with social content strategy
- Experience with influencer analytics or creator performance tracking

### Roles and Responsibilities
- Monitor and extract social media performance data across platforms (Meta, Instagram, LinkedIn, X/Twitter, YouTube, TikTok) using native analytics tools and social listening platforms
- Build and maintain regular social media performance reports and dashboards tracking reach, engagement, impressions, follower growth, and sentiment
- Conduct audience analysis to understand demographics, interests, and content preferences of brand followers
- Perform content performance analysis to identify top-performing post types, formats, and themes
- Track brand sentiment and share of voice using social listening tools (e.g. Brandwatch, Sprinklr, Meltwater, or similar)
- Conduct competitive benchmarking to identify opportunities and gaps relative to key competitors
- Support campaign reporting for paid and organic social, providing pre- and post-campaign analysis
- Collaborate with content, community management, and paid media teams to align analytics outputs with strategic needs
- Translate social insights into clear, actionable recommendations for non-technical stakeholders
- Stay updated on platform algorithm changes, new features, and emerging trends in social media
